Four types of systems created 
1. Gaging System: Find out what specific posts and topics when mentioned across multiple accounts bring the MOST views and likes to accounts with low history, and replicate those. Find out what businesses and app ideas bring the most revenue when replicated 
2. Prospect finder: Take a need idea topic, and use search keywords dynamically live generated by AI to find the specific prospects that are actively searching for a solution, through forums and more places, monitoring, and also find the best icp across multiple respondents to target with a business idea 
3. Profiling and cross target: use social media to find people who are in need of solutions, and track them across multiple places and accounts, using different methods to hit them from different angles 
4. Website landing page creation: Create landing pages and sign up systems and basic apps to gage whether or not a solution should be pursued in an area. 

Three Specific Workflows Created 

1. Argon: Studying Social media posts & recreating 
2. Krypton: Client generation & prospecting 
3. Xenon: Validation, building & outreach 

We are not going to start with Argon, we are going to start with Krypton.

4. LinkedIn


Part A: Profile/Post/Data handler 
Profiles

* Local data sink 
* Playright controls:
   * Fetch profile image -> Save 
   * Fetch header image -> Save 
   * Fetch profile details (expand “more” under the about section, scroll furthest down possible, copy ALL page text without parsing/editing & save to text file)
   * If featured section exists: 
      * Get all elements inside 
      * Right click all resources/posts
      * If website contained, save for that person as website needed to be seen 
   * If activity section exists
      * Click show all button 
      * Click “Load more”, scroll down, find load more, click & repeat again, until it has been done 10 times 
      * Beside EACH post, click the three dots, and click copy link to post, send each post link to Post processor and receive 
   * Example profiles
      * Featured & Activity sections:
https://www.linkedin.com/in/fraser-cottrell/recent-activity/all/


Posts

* Open post link 
* Record metrics (Likes, comments, repost, share)
* Open comments, for each comment, record comment details, profile url (RC -> Copy) 
* Click on reaction history button (should look like mini symbols of the metrics), then copy the names of each person and reactions they had. 



Part B: Search terms 
Take AI search terms -> paste them into the LinkedIn search 
Filters:

1. People

* Copy all results as appeared and parse into a list with name description and intent 
* Paginate all the way to the end, and for each person record what listed page they appeared on 
* Paste all lists to GPT-6.1-sol-medium OR Claude Sonnet 5.5 Medium with the statement Return ALL that do not appear to provide the service we are looking for searching for (keyword) to find people that need that service. Up to 10 per prompt. 
* All that are returned are parsed through the system & saved

2. Jobs
   1. For each search term, Do one search per location specified in beginning variables
   2. Click and paginate on all jobs and record each one, the company link, the full control A including the details of the job. Do not click apply under any circumstances. 
3. Posts
   1. Expand all posts and send each one to the post processor. Hit load more 20 times
4. Groups
   1. Ignore groups for now but we will surely come back HERE.
